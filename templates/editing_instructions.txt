Military documentary editing instructions

- Prefer relevant visuals that directly support the narration.
- Mix video footage and still photographs when appropriate.
- Keep source attribution and asset metadata with every selected item.
- Check each DVIDS asset's specific copyright/usage notice before publication.
- Do not imply endorsement by the U.S. Department of Defense or any military branch.
- Favor clear, documentary-style pacing over random montage.
- Avoid repetitive shots and avoid claiming footage depicts a specific event unless the source supports it.
